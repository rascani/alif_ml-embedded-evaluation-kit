Measurement

Each boot: one separate first call, 10 warm-ups, 100 measured calls. Input preparation, reporting and output checks are outside timing; caches are not flushed. All 24 selected boots and 12 ET saved-output checks pass. The final complete TFLM pass is used. Independent hardware timer cross-checking remains pending.

Accounted inference RAM

Used arena/pools plus persistent runtime/kernel allocations and outside state; ET includes attributed runtime static storage. Excludes unused reservations, initialization workspace, stack high water, transient heap peaks and benchmark/platform/reporting memory. These are component costs, not whole-device requirements.

ET DS-CNN uses 20,336 fewer RAM bytes than the previous ET build. Both frameworks now have identical planned/head sizes for all four models. Code/models remain in MRAM, inference pools in SRAM, and globals/heap/stack in DTCM.
